Jutsu Hero — Naruto Hand Seal Rhythm Game

Jutsu Hero is a free, browser-based rhythm game where you perform Naruto-style hand seals in front of your webcam. A real-time computer-vision model (MediaPipe Hands + a custom-trained ONNX classifier) recognizes each of the 12 zodiac hand seals and turns correct sequences into jutsus — Kage Bunshin no Jutsu, Chidori, Katon: Goukakyuu no Jutsu, and more. No download, no account, no backend required: it runs entirely in your browser.

Game modes

  • Single Player — practice hand seals against the clock.
  • Local Battle — two players, split-screen, same device.
  • Online Battle — create a room, share a code, and fight friends in real time from separate devices (1v1 or 4-player battle royale).

Frequently asked questions

What is Jutsu Hero?

Jutsu Hero is a free, browser-based rhythm game where you perform Naruto-style hand seals in front of your webcam. A real-time computer-vision model recognizes each seal and translates correct sequences into jutsus.

How does Jutsu Hero recognize hand seals?

It uses MediaPipe Hands to track 21 hand landmarks from your webcam feed, then a custom-trained ONNX classifier predicts which of the 12 zodiac hand seals you are forming, all running client-side in the browser with no backend required.

Is Jutsu Hero free to play?

Yes. Jutsu Hero is completely free, requires no download or account, and runs directly in a modern browser at jutsu-hero.bunkerapps.net.

Can I play Jutsu Hero online with friends?

Yes. Online Battle mode lets you create a room, share a short code with a friend, and fight in real time from separate devices, including 1v1 and 4-player battle royale.

Is Jutsu Hero affiliated with or endorsed by the Naruto franchise?

No. Jutsu Hero is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by Masashi Kishimoto, Shueisha, Studio Pierrot, VIZ Media, or Bandai Namco. Naruto is the property of its respective owners.

Do I need a webcam to play Jutsu Hero?

A webcam is recommended for the full hand-seal experience, but Jutsu Hero also has a full keyboard fallback (keys 1-8) for players without camera access.
